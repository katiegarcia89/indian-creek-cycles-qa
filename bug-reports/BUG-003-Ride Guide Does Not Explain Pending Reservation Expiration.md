# BUG-003: Ride Guide Does Not Explain Pending Reservation Expiration

## Status

Open

## Severity

Low

## Priority

Medium

## Module

Ride Guide / Customer Information

## Description

The Ride Guide does not explain how long a reservation may remain in Pending status or when an unpaid reservation will be cancelled.

## Expected Result

The Ride Guide should explain the pending reservation grace period and notify customers that unpaid reservations may be automatically cancelled after 24 hours past the scheduled start time.

## Actual Result

Customers are not given information about the expiration or cancellation policy for pending reservations.

## Impact

Customers may not understand how long their reservation will be held or why it was automatically cancelled.

## Suggested Fix

Add the pending reservation policy to the Ride Guide, including:

- How long a reservation may remain pending
- When automatic cancellation occurs
- How customers can request an exception
